## The entry

> My startup has 40,000 daily active users perched on every statue downtown, zero churn, and I'm raising a seed round — literally, birdseed — at a CAC of one crumb.

## Why it fits the judge brief

- **It is a one-line elevator pitch from a city pigeon to investors.** The speaker is a founder pigeon, the audience is investors, and the line is the ask: a seed round.
- **It has a real joke that lands, not a bare pun.** Three jokes stack in one sentence: the "seed round" is literally birdseed, the customer-acquisition cost is a single crumb, and the traction claim — 40,000 daily active users — is true in the most pigeon way possible, because the users are just pigeons sitting on statues. The line even talks like a pitch deck ("daily active users", "zero churn", "CAC") while every metric is secretly about birds on statues.
- **It is original and surprising.** The twist is that the startup's impressive metrics are all literally true of ordinary pigeon life: the users never leave, the churn is zero because they are perched there, and growth costs crumbs.
- **Only a pigeon could say it.** Statues downtown, birdseed, crumbs, perching — every image is city-pigeon life, and the pitch collapses without it.
- **It fits one line in English, well within 200 characters**, and every word earns its place: traction, retention, and the ask, with no filler.

## How it runs

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the single entry line to `/output/entry.txt` (creating `/output` if needed), as UTF-8 text with no trailing newline.
